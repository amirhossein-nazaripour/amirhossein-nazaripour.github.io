About

About me

I am a pharmacist, trained at Tehran University of Medical Sciences (Pharm.D), with an MBA from Sharif University of Technology. In October 2023 I founded MAKU, a healthcare marketing company in Tehran. A team of about 30 people works across its four units: Maku Marketing, Maku Studio (branding), Maku Vision (market research) and Maku College (training).

My work has shown me Iran's pharmaceutical industry from several sides: marketing and sales, parts of regulation and production, and more recently the financial statements of the companies themselves. Across all of it I see the same thing: decisions in this industry are rarely based on market data. Most of my work is aimed at changing that.

Focus

What I work on

  • Market research & data

    Turning Iran's official drug statistics into answers to practical questions: market size, market share, trends, and how events such as the 2025 war shift demand.

  • Marketing & commercialization

    Positioning, launch planning and sales strategy for pharmaceutical and health products, built on what the market data shows.

  • Financial reading of pharma companies

    Reading the financial statements of Iranian pharmaceutical companies alongside their market performance, to see how commercial results show up in the numbers.

  • Teaching

    Courses and webinars on market research and sales for people working in the pharmaceutical and health industry.

For investors & companies

Looking at Iran's healthcare market?

Iran has a large pharmaceutical industry that is mostly domestic. Most of the information about it is in Persian, scattered across sources, or hard to read from outside. If you are assessing the market as an investor, a manufacturer or a potential partner, I can help you understand how it works: the data, the main companies, how products are marketed and sold, and what the numbers suggest about where it is heading.

A short email describing what you are looking at is the best place to start.
